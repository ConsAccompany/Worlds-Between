Scope
-----
Fixed-score instrumental themes for four Worlds Between contexts: city
(76 BPM), field (92 BPM), ruins (62 BPM), and battle (116 BPM). Each theme is
sixteen bars and has an editable score JSON, a standard MIDI file, and a
24 kHz / 16-bit / stereo PCM WAV rendered by the Python source in this package.
