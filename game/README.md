# Worlds Between — MVP game project

Open `game/project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2. Run the project, choose "开始探索" / "Start exploring", set the protagonist's name and appearance, and either open the brief field guide or skip it. On the science-region map, visit the three destinations in any order. Move with WASD or arrow keys, press E near people and consoles, and use Esc to return to the map. Each destination has a field-survey entrance leading to two rooms. Inspect six clues in each survey, connect them into three evidence pairs at the analysis console, then answer its final question. The **Clue notes** button at the analysis console lets you reread collected clue text without losing an evidence selection or leaving the final judgment. Incorrect pairs and answers can be retried; completion grants JP once. Check the relay in the outskirts and the records console in the ruins. Observe a machine by bypassing it, or touch one to enter combat. Return to the city's report desk after the field log is complete to reach the demo endpoint; you can continue exploring afterward. The **Field log** button shows objectives, survey progress, discovered records and run time in Chinese or English.

In battle, move with A/D or left/right arrows, press J to attack, K to dodge, and hold L to guard. Winning returns you to the same area with 20%-of-maximum HP and energy recovery; defeat returns to the main menu. Leaving and reentering a location resets its enemy.

From the world map's **Field log**, open **Party talk** after inviting a companion. Six optional bilingual conversations unlock from survey completion and the recruited party: three individual reflections and three conversations between companions. Topics can be replayed or reached later in any order. Finishing one for the first time records it, raises each participant's prototype bond by 3, and autosaves; replays do not grant more bond.

Recruit Liang Xu in the city, Zhao Ling in the outskirts, and Ji Cheng in the ruins after examining the records console. Choosing “later” leaves each available on a return visit. Only the protagonist is shown while exploring. In battle, Tab switches controlled characters, Q uses their personal skill, R uses their current job skill, P pauses to choose any unlocked skill in the current job series or issue temporary teammate orders, and T toggles full auto. The pause panel also cycles each teammate's persistent balanced, attack, or cautious AI policy.

Each location also has one optional field station. After recruitment, Liang Xu can compare the city's allocation ledger, Zhao Ling can trace the outskirts' echo node, and Ji Cheng can read the ruins' strata panel. You can examine a station before recruiting its specialist and return later. The first successful use adds a finding to the bilingual field log, grants that companion 4 JP and 2 bond, and autosaves; repeating it grants nothing. These stations do not block the city report.

Victory awards each active character XP and JP. A traveling reserve receives 25% of both. Levels are individual and restore full HP and energy. At every level divisible by 10, the character must choose to stay, promote (when the next MVP stage exists), or enter another series allowed by their aptitude. The game opens the growth screen after a battle at a threshold; it can also be opened from the world map. Four job series have their first two stages and two skills each. Learned ranks and job stages remain recorded when switching away and back. JP upgrades unlocked job and personal skills; higher aptitude lowers JP cost by up to 50%. The MVP starts the protagonist at level 1. Job art and skill effects remain temporary.

Use **Save** on the world map, in an exploration area, or in a survey room to choose one of three manual slots. **Load Game** on the main menu opens the same list. Autosaves retain the five most recent records: contact with an enemy creates a safe prebattle checkpoint, victory writes a second record after recovery and rewards, and survey clues, evidence links, results and completed party talks also save. Defeat returns to the main menu, where the checkpoint can be loaded. Saves use version 1 JSON in Godot's `user://saves` directory; an unsupported version is shown but cannot be loaded. Exploration location, precise hero position, survey room, clues and evidence links, visible/cleared enemy, discovery and recruitment state, party, conversation progress, prototype bonds, individual growth and job records all restore. A pending mandatory job choice opens the growth screen before exploration resumes.

## Project structure

- `scenes/` — menu, protagonist setup, world map, three configurable exploration locations, six survey rooms, fixed battle arena, report endpoint, and temporary foundation room
- `scripts/` — app language and input state, menu, map, exploration, combat rules, battle arena, and room behavior
- `assets/characters/` and `assets/audio/` — uniquely named prototype sprites, ambient loops and discovery cue

Stable content IDs will use `board.area.object` (for example, `scifi.outskirts.recruit_zhaoling`). Save data and content registries begin in later MVP steps. The four aptitude IDs are `martial`, `adaptive`, `scholarly`, and `spiritual`; naming IDs in English keeps localization text separate from saved identifiers.

Run `--headless --path game --script res://tests/m1_smoke.gd` through `res://tests/m7_smoke.gd` with Godot for milestone smoke checks. After M6, run `res://tests/m6_restart_smoke.gd` in a separate Godot process to verify a cold load. Test saves use isolated `user://test-saves` folders. Machine bypass is recorded only when the player moves into the bypass area; returning from a survey or loading within it does not count by itself. Each destination has two optional field traces; find both and bring its local companion to the station to interpret them. Exploration and combat figures remain temporary geometric art. M7's route works, but its 25–35 minute duration has not been timed or verified yet.

The launcher opens the bundled Godot game's visible window. Start a new game, play through the city report, then close the game window. The launcher copies only a new, validated JSON playtest receipt into `outputs/`; the bible then shows its status and summary. That receipt records elapsed time, cumulative time per destination, survey completion points, location and milestone order, recruits, optional field traces, surveys, conversations completed before the report, and incorrect evidence and judgment attempts. It contains no protagonist name. An automated smoke run can validate the receipt format, but its elapsed time is not a human playthrough. The launcher requires the local `work/tooling/godot-4-7-2` executable and therefore is not a standalone Windows export.
