At game scale, v08 improves all five issues that prompted the pass. The raised sword reads as one bright diagonal rather than v07's short dashed stroke. The tan fist sits above the belt and is separated from it; a teal hip strip and lit hem help the upper coat separate from ruins paving. The back attachment is now a roll above the tan pack rather than a square waist pouch. A wider staggered stance avoids the parallel-boot look. The high ponytail, teal scarf and character spacing remain readable among four party members.

It is still a static pilot. The teal roll nearly joins the scarf in silhouette and reads more like a shoulder block than a clearly coiled bedroll. The dark far leg and boots blend into both floors, especially in ruins. The narrow hip drape does not carry as much of the approved exploration v8's back-fabric character. The weapon reads better, though it remains small at 1×. The other three party sprites look visibly rougher beside the protagonist. Refine leg/floor separation, roll/scarf spacing and cross-view fabric identity before treating this as a motion baseline or asking for art acceptance.
