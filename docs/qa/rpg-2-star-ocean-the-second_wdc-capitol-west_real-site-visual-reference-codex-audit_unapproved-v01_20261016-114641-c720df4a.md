The real [Architect of the Capitol building page](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/buildings-grounds/capitol-building) gives the building's north-to-south length as 751 ft 4 in and its height to the Statue of Freedom as 288 ft. This is an architectural reference, not a required game-world pixel ratio: v05's compact backdrop should still read as a monumental structure behind the 48 px heroine.

The official [Capitol Grounds history](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/buildings-grounds/capitol-grounds) explains that, seen from the west, the earlier earthen terraces made the building seem inadequately supported at the top of its hill, so Olmsted added marble terraces on the north, west and south sides to give it stability and repose. The [Reflecting Pool page](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/buildings-grounds/capitol-grounds/reflecting-pool) places the pool at the eastern end of the National Mall, occupying over half of Union Square, set into a plaza that includes the Ulysses S. Grant memorial on its east. Do not compress the pool, terrace and Capitol into one immediately walkable patch without deliberate scene transitions.

At the current v05 entry camera, the dome and horizontal wings are recognizable, but repeated equal-height bands in the lawn, steps and facade make the ruins feel diagrammatic. The Capitol should retain a stronger dominant silhouette, with more varied broken edges and a clearer rise from west lawn to terraces. Keep the creator-approved recognizable building outline, localized collapse, vegetation invasion, science survey station and patrol machines. Do not treat a visual reference note as scene acceptance or raise 0/11.
