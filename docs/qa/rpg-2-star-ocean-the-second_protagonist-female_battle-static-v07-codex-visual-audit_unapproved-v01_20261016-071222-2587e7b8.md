- Her ponytail is now a clear high, long silhouette without the teal roll intersecting it. The darker collar wraps the scarf more clearly. The pack emblem and cuff remain distinguishable at battle size.
- The shorter pack simplifies the shoulder outline, and the green trouser leg makes a visible break below the dark coat. The individual boots are easier to count.
