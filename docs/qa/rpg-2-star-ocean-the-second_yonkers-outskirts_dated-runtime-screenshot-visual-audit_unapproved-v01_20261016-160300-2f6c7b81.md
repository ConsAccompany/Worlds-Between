## Visible observations

- The path, circular ornamental paving, flower beds, fountain, greenhouse-like structure and canvas-roofed stall form a polished garden/plaza image. They do not visually establish the creator-decided Yonkers, New York, science-sphere outskirts.
- The environment carries much denser texture and finer shading than the small player and NPC sprites. The brown central enemy is visibly a blocky placeholder against that background.
- Large labels and dark circular markers cover several focal areas. The machine, intermediate station and annex entrance do remain identifiable, but the scenery and gameplay markers compete for attention.
- This one static image does not establish collision, animation, entrance legibility during movement, or performance.
