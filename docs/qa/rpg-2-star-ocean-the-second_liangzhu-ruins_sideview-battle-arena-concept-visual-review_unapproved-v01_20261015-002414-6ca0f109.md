## Visible review

- The image uses a genuine side view with an open, nearly continuous dirt combat strip through the lower middle. The skyline, earthen terraces, wetland, wooden footbridge and small survey station place the arena in the same visual family as the separate Liangzhu exploration concept.
- It removes the classical stone columns, statue and dark tiled plaza currently shown in the Godot battle arena. The backdrop is visually distinct from the existing science city.
- Survey equipment is small enough to leave the archaeological terrain as the primary subject. There are no interface elements, characters, or enemies baked into the image.
- The foreground fence and reeds sit below the likely sprite feet. They might still compete with the game's bottom status panel after crop and need an actual 1280×720 runtime check.
- The bronze sentry's outline against the ochre ground is **not yet verified**. Its cyan eye and dark rim may carry the contrast, but moving battle sprites and telegraphs need inspection at native scale before selection.

## Integration gate

1. Preview this candidate in the fixed arena at 1280×720 with all five party slots, sentry idle/attack, telegraph, HUD, Chinese and English labels.
2. Check the flat combat line, feet grounding, sprite contrast and obstruction throughout the sentry's full animation, including near-contact poses.
3. Confirm the image can be cropped/scaled without changing apparent character size or undermining the horizontal combat layout.
