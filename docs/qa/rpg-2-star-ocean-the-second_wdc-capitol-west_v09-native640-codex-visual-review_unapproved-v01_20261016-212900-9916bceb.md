## What improved from v08

At game size, the right wing is now unmistakably damaged instead of looking like a sound public building with a separate stone pile. The Capitol dome and central pavilion remain identifiable. A teal-and-white survey outpost on the left is visible at 1× and reads separately from the red/orange patrol machine. The heroine's route and the camera composition appear unchanged in the sampled entry, vista and occlusion frames. This also makes Washington more distinct from the functioning NYC City Hall Park scene.

## Remaining visual blocker

The breach is a broad near-black staircase silhouette with repeated square tops. In the 3× crop, it reads more like an empty cutout pasted across the facade than a multi-plane collapsed building: few inner wall faces or fallen architectural pieces bridge the gap from the broken upper story to the terrace. The dark mass dominates the right half at 1×, while the patrol machine and pale rubble meet along its lower edge. The screen is clearer as a ruin, but its main damage still lacks the authored material depth required by the project's environment target.

For the next art pass, keep the asymmetric missing span and left survey accent, then break the dark area into a smaller deep opening, one or two lit inner masonry planes, staggered column fragments, exposed terrace core and a believable rubble fall. The machine must stand in front of readable ground, not merge with the void. Keep the central dome, the 48-pixel heroine route, walkmask and collision untouched. Review the change first at native 640×360; zoomed crops can diagnose pixel edges but cannot prove the gameplay read. This is a recommendation for a later candidate, not permission to mark v09 accepted or install it as runtime art.
