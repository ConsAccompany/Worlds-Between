v06 makes the Capitol and dome larger behind the 48 px heroine; the building silhouette is immediately recognizable at 640×360. The break in the right wing is less sawtooth-like than v05. The distinct evergreens and larger dark patrol machine also make more of the 2537 science-survey premise readable without labels. The central approach, survey apparatus and beacon remain legible.

The closer camera compresses the West Lawn into a narrow horizontal strip between the terrace and foreground plaza. This weakens the sense of walking through the grounds and leaves the landmark looking like a painted stage wall. The facade repeats nearly identical windows and columns across the full width; the foreground paving, meadow and rubble still have repeated procedural patterns. The dark patrol machine reads as one large backdrop shape at 640×360, without an evident ground contact or motion cue. The large white cloud bank at the right of the 1280×720 vista competes with the dome. The scene is a useful composition and walkability pilot but not yet the requested finished environment quality.

For the next pass, prioritize a visible transition from plaza through West Lawn to rising terraces, more hand-placed asymmetry in collapse/material wear, and distinct grounded forms for rubble and patrol machine. Retain the larger recognizable dome but avoid solving scale by flattening the traversable depth. A creator choice may be needed if a single 640×360 screen cannot show both monument scale and a deep lawn; keep this unresolved while the creator is away.
