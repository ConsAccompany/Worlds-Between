# Character sprite direction: Sea of Stars

Recorded: 2026-10-20T18:10:26.112Z (recording time, not the exact message timestamp).

## Creator decision

> sea of stars, that's the style we are going with

The creator selected expressive, stylized pixel characters with Sea of Stars as the visual reference. This replaces the detailed classic JRPG sprite direction for future character production. Reference: https://seaofstarsgame.co/ . Borrow the broad visual principles; Worlds Between keeps its original characters, costume identities and setting.

## Sprite contract

- Build clear silhouettes, expressive heads and poses, distinct hair and costume shapes, and broad coherent color clusters.
- Use controlled light/shadow planes and restrained surface detail. Remove noisy texture and isolated decorative pixels when they obscure form at normal gameplay size.
- Preserve the protagonist's established brown ponytail, identity and clothing language; the existing approved south idle is an identity reference, not evidence that a redesigned set has been accepted.
- Judge faces and body proportions in actual gameplay. Larger source dimensions and extra detail alone do not demonstrate improved readability.
- Keep motion smooth, fluid and well-paced through anticipation, push-off, landing, contact and recovery; inspect start/run/stop/re-press/reversal at normal speed. Frame count alone is not a quality criterion.
- Retain the approved 48-logical-pixel exploration display scale and battle node scale 0.65 for the initial comparison. If the target cannot read at these sizes, show a separately labeled scale alternative for creator approval before adopting it.
- Preserve editable layered Pixelorama sources, explicit pivots, deterministic exports and source-bound actual Godot captures.
